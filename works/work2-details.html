ForceField

— Visualizing Intermaterial Interaction through Floor and Depth Sensing

Abstract

ForceField realizes the measurement of pervasive and intermaterial contact forces across any rigid surface within a room-scale environment, without the need for additional device attachments to bodies or objects. The visualization of the force vectors is achieved by acquiring 3D geometry data through a depth sensor and capturing ground reaction force distribution through a floor sensor; our sensor-fusion algorithm then reconstructs the contact forces between material surfaces without any attached sensors. ForceField opens up the possibility of highly physical spatial interactions in domains such as healthcare, sports, and entertainment.
