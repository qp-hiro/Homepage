Heteromorphic Locomotion Interface

— Transformation to universal creatures

Abstract

This study investigates a locomotion interface that provides general feedback on the physical characteristics of an animal avatar in an Immersive Virtual Environment (IVE) independently of vision. By presenting the periodicity in the locomotion behavior of the immersed animal using a force-sensing presentation device — and encouraging the operator to move their body accordingly — we aim to induce the embodied sensation and psychological effects of wearing an avatar.
