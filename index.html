    Profile プロフィール

    2025 · Tokyo

    Born 2000 in Fukuoka, Japan. Spent his childhood across Japan and China. Studied in the Faculty of Engineering at The University of Tokyo, continued through the Graduate School of Information Science and Technology, and is currently a doctoral student in Advanced Interdisciplinary Studies at UTokyo, RCAST. 2000年福岡県福岡市生まれ。幼少期を日中の両国で過ごす。東京大学工学部を卒業後、同大学院情報理工学系研究科を経て、現在は先端学際工学専攻の博士課程に在籍。

    03 / Research & Skills 研究と技能

    Research & Skills 研究分野と技能

    My work spans a wide range of related fields — VR / AR, robotics, haptics, biomechanics, motor learning, and AI.

    Through implementations and exhibitions, I take up the question: how does an extended body reshape perception and action? VR/AR, ロボティクス, ハプティクス, バイオメカニクス, 運動学習, AIなど、関連領域は多岐に渡る。拡張された身体が、知覚や行為をいかに変えるのかという問いに、実装・展示を通じて取り組んでいます。
